# Site Discovery Skill

## Purpose

Convert an initial brief and available source material into reliable design and planning inputs.

Discovery should understand the organisation before proposing pages or aesthetics.

## Source Priority

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. explicit current user requirements
2. supplied current business information and brand assets
3. authoritative organisation-owned sources
4. existing website content
5. clearly labelled assumptions

Do not treat inferred or third-party claims as confirmed business facts.

## 1. Organisation

Establish:

- what the organisation is
- what it offers
- where and how it operates
- what makes it meaningfully different
- any constraints or regulated claims

Avoid marketing embellishment not supported by evidence.

## 2. Audience

Identify major audience groups from evidence.

For each, determine:

- likely reason for visiting
- information required
- likely action
- uncertainty or friction

Do not create elaborate fictional personas unless requested.

## 3. Goals and Conversions

Separate organisation goals from user goals.

Identify the few actions that matter most, such as:

- enquiry
- booking
- purchase
- visit
- phone call
- quote request
- application
- information retrieval

Do not make every possible action a primary conversion.

## 4. Content Inventory

Record available content by type:

- identity and brand
- products/services
- about/history
- team
- locations
- contact details
- pricing
- menus/catalogues
- FAQs
- policies
- testimonials/reviews
- imagery/video
- credentials
- case studies

Mark each item as confirmed, incomplete, missing, duplicated or outdated where evidence permits.

## 5. Brand Signals

Extract concrete signals rather than vague adjectives.

Consider:

- language and tone
- existing typography
- colour
- photography
- physical environment
- packaging/signage
- audience expectations
- price positioning
- cultural or historical cues

Distinguish existing brand requirements from possible future design directions.

## 6. Existing Website Review

When an existing site is available, inspect it as evidence rather than as a template to reproduce.

Identify:

- useful content worth preserving
- user journeys
- navigation
- functional requirements
- SEO-sensitive URLs/content
- weak hierarchy
- outdated material
- duplication
- accessibility issues
- strong distinctive elements

Do not automatically copy the existing layout.

## 7. Asset Audit

List available:

- logos
- brand marks
- fonts/licences
- photographs
- illustrations
- icons
- video
- documents
- maps or location material

Record quality, dimensions and likely usability where known.

Do not assume an asset is licensed for use merely because it can be found online.

## 8. Functional Requirements

Identify actual functionality required, for example:

- booking
- commerce
- forms
- maps
- menus
- filtering
- search
- authentication
- CMS editing
- integrations

Separate launch-critical requirements from future possibilities.

## 9. Constraints

Record constraints such as:

- technology
- hosting
- accessibility
- performance
- budget/maintenance
- content availability
- legal/privacy requirements
- deadlines
- third-party systems

Do not design functionality that cannot realistically be maintained.

## 10. Unknowns

Create an explicit unknowns list.

Classify unknowns as:

- blocking
- important but non-blocking
- safe to defer

Use conservative placeholders or assumptions only when progress does not risk factual misrepresentation.

## Discovery Deliverable

Produce a concise discovery brief containing:

- organisation summary
- evidence/source notes
- audience groups
- site goals
- primary actions
- content inventory
- brand signals
- assets
- functionality
- constraints
- unknowns
- assumptions
- material from an existing site worth preserving

Pass this output to site planning.

## Completion Standard

Discovery is complete when site planning can proceed without guessing the organisation's basic purpose, audience, principal actions, available content, brand evidence or major constraints.
