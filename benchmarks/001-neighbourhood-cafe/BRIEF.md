# Benchmark 001 — Neighbourhood Café

## Purpose

A controlled benchmark for testing whether the Website Generator can produce a distinctive small-business website without falling into generic AI landing-page patterns.

This is a fictional business. No factual claims from this benchmark should be reused for a real client.

## Business

**Name:** Alder & Lane  
**Type:** Independent neighbourhood café  
**Location:** Inner-city Australian neighbourhood  
**Positioning:** Warm, food-led local café with strong coffee, seasonal breakfast/lunch and a relaxed but considered identity.

## Audience

- nearby residents
- weekend breakfast visitors
- local workers
- people checking menu, hours and location before visiting

## Primary user goals

1. Understand what kind of café it is.
2. See food and atmosphere.
3. Check opening hours and location.
4. View the current menu.
5. Decide to visit.

## Business goals

1. Increase in-person visits.
2. Make practical information effortless to find.
3. Communicate a distinctive identity without appearing expensive or corporate.

## Required pages

- Home
- Menu
- Visit

Do not add About, Journal, Shop, FAQ or other pages unless discovery/planning produces a genuine need.

## Required content

### Home

- café name and concise positioning
- strong food/interior imagery
- current opening hours
- short description of food and coffee
- selected menu highlights
- location
- clear route to full menu and visit information

### Menu

- breakfast
- lunch
- pastries
- coffee and non-alcoholic drinks
- dietary indicators where appropriate

### Visit

- address
- opening hours
- transport/parking note
- accessibility note
- map/location treatment
- contact details

## Brand signals

The site should feel:

- warm
- tactile
- local
- confident
- unfussy
- contemporary without resembling a technology startup

Avoid default café clichés such as faux-handwritten typography, coffee-bean graphics, brown-on-cream rustic templates or excessive vintage decoration.

## Visual assets

Assume a useful set of high-quality photographs exists:

- exterior
- interior
- plated breakfast
- sandwich/lunch
- pastry
- coffee
- people using the space

The composition should be image-aware from the beginning.

## Constraints

- mobile use is important
- practical information must be quickly accessible
- no online ordering
- no account/login
- no newsletter
- no fabricated awards, reviews or statistics
- accessibility target: WCAG 2.2 AA
- performance should suit mobile connections

## Benchmark Questions

After generation, evaluate:

1. Could the same design be used for a dentist, consultant or SaaS product merely by changing copy and colours?
2. Does the page feel designed around café content and imagery?
3. Is practical visit information easy to find?
4. Are cards, pills, gradients and rounded boxes being used without reason?
5. Does mobile feel intentionally composed?
6. Does the typography have character without harming readability?
7. Does section rhythm feel deliberate rather than templated?
8. Does the design remain coherent across all three pages?

## Pass Condition

The benchmark passes only after the full generator workflow has been used:

Discovery → Planning → Design System → Page Composition → Implementation → Responsive Review → Visual Review → Refinement → Accessibility → Final Validation.
