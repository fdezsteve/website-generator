# Alder & Lane — Page Composition

## Home
Page job: make the café immediately understandable, desirable and easy to visit.

1. Opening frame — café name/positioning integrated with a dominant food/interior photographic composition rather than a conventional boxed hero. Hours/location are visible in the first useful viewport. “See the menu” is the single emphasized route.
2. Food rhythm — a short editorial sequence pairing one breakfast image, one lunch image and concise copy about seasonal breakfast/lunch. Layout shifts scale rather than repeating cards.
3. Menu glimpse — a small set of representative menu lines in real menu typography, adjacent to food imagery. The list itself is interactive in the interaction spec; full Menu remains obvious.
4. Place — environmental/people imagery and a brief statement about the neighbourhood experience. Let imagery interrupt the grid.
5. Visit handoff — address/hours and route to Visit, visually integrated rather than a generic CTA banner.

Mobile transformation: practical hours/location rise immediately beneath identity; the opening photograph remains substantial; paired desktop imagery becomes a deliberate alternating sequence; menu interaction becomes tap-first.

## Menu
Page job: let visitors understand the food quickly while making browsing feel like part of the café experience.

1. Menu masthead — compact identity and a clear current-menu framing, not another oversized hero.
2. Category navigator — Breakfast / Lunch / Pastries / Drinks; sticky only if testing shows it improves long-menu navigation.
3. Menu field — typographic menu rows grouped by category with description, price and dietary notation when benchmark data exists.
4. Context image rail — food imagery changes or advances in relationship to the category/item being explored; it must never obscure the menu.
5. Visit bridge — hours and “Plan your visit” after the visitor has enough menu context.

Mobile transformation: category controls remain reachable and horizontally manageable without page-level overflow; imagery appears at meaningful category transitions rather than consuming space beside every item.

## Visit
Page job: remove uncertainty between deciding to go and arriving.

1. Immediate practical block — address and today's/full opening hours given strongest hierarchy.
2. Location field — map/location treatment and exterior image form an orientation composition rather than a generic embedded-map card.
3. Arrival notes — transport/parking and accessibility presented as readable editorial notes with clear headings.
4. Contact — concise contact information and return route to Menu where useful.

Mobile transformation: address/hours precede large media; direction/contact controls are easy to tap; map treatment cannot trap page scrolling.

## Cross-page rhythm
Home is immersive, Menu is typographic/interactive, Visit is practical/spatial. Shared typography, grid, rules and image treatment create coherence without making the pages use the same section stack.
