# Alder & Lane — Discovery

## Organisation summary
Alder & Lane is a fictional independent neighbourhood café in an inner-city Australian neighbourhood. It is food-led, with strong coffee, seasonal breakfast and lunch, and a relaxed but considered identity. This benchmark exists to test generator quality; its facts must not be reused for a real client.

## Evidence
Source: benchmark brief only. No existing site or external business source is assumed.

## Audiences
- Nearby residents: want a dependable local place and fast practical information.
- Weekend breakfast visitors: want atmosphere, food cues, menu and confidence that the trip is worthwhile.
- Local workers: want hours, lunch options and location quickly.
- Intent-led visitors: arrive mainly to check menu, hours or directions.

## Site goals
Drive in-person visits, make practical information effortless to retrieve, and establish a distinctive local identity without appearing expensive, corporate or generic.

## Primary actions
1. Decide to visit.
2. View the menu.
3. Check hours and location.

There is no online ordering, account, newsletter or fabricated conversion mechanism.

## Content inventory
Confirmed categories: café positioning, breakfast, lunch, pastries, coffee/non-alcoholic drinks, dietary indicators, opening hours, address, transport/parking, accessibility, contact details, and photography of exterior/interior/food/coffee/people.

Exact menu items, prices, address, hours, contact details and transport/accessibility wording are not supplied. They must remain clearly fictional benchmark content or placeholders until benchmark data is explicitly defined.

## Brand signals
Warm, tactile, local, confident, unfussy and contemporary. Food and physical place should carry the identity. Avoid faux-handwriting, coffee-bean motifs, brown/cream rustic cliché, excessive vintage styling and polished technology-startup aesthetics.

## Asset audit
Assume high-quality photographs covering exterior, interior, plated breakfast, sandwich/lunch, pastry, coffee and people using the space. Actual image files are not yet supplied. No logo, font licence, illustration set or video is confirmed.

## Functionality
Launch-critical: responsive navigation, accessible menu exploration, clear visit information and location treatment. Rich interaction is desirable where it strengthens menu/imagery exploration. No commerce, authentication or newsletter.

## Constraints
WCAG 2.2 AA target; mobile-first practical use; mobile-connection performance; no fabricated awards/reviews/statistics; three-page scope unless a real need emerges.

## Unknowns
Important but non-blocking: exact address, hours, menu/prices, contact details, dietary data, transport/parking, accessibility statement, logo/wordmark and actual photography.

Safe to defer: future CMS/editorial workflow and real map integration.

## Assumptions
For design benchmarking, missing business facts may be represented by explicitly fictional test data. Design must not depend on invented social proof or business history.

## Discovery outcome
Planning can proceed. The defining tension is deliberate: practical local information must be immediate, while food, photography and interaction must make the site feel authored rather than like a generic café template.
